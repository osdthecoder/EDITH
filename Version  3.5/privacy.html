EDITH Privacy Policy

EDITH by OBSYRA · Last updated: 12 November 2026

In short

EDITH processes your PDFs and images on your own device. We do not upload your files, create accounts, or run analytics or advertising.

Your files

When you open a file in EDITH, it is read and processed by your browser. The result is created on your device and saved only when you choose to download it. Your file contents are never sent to us or to any other server by EDITH.

What EDITH stores on your device

  • A history of processed files: file name, size, format and date. File contents are not stored.
  • A count of how often you use each tool, to show frequently used tools.
  • Saved copies of the app and its libraries, so it can open offline.

This data stays in your browser. You can remove it at any time with Settings → Clear local data, File History → Clear history, or by clearing the site's data in your browser. File names can be personal, so clear the history on shared devices.

Third parties

  • Code libraries. EDITH loads open-source libraries (pdf-lib, PDF.js, JSZip) from cdnjs.cloudflare.com. When your browser fetches them, that service can see standard connection details such as your IP address. Your files are not part of those requests.
  • Hosting. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like most web hosts, GitHub may log standard visit details. See GitHub's own privacy statement for how it handles them.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect your name, email, phone number or location.
  • We do not sell or share personal data.
  • We do not send your documents to AI services.

Children

EDITH is a general utility and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change too.

Contact

Questions or concerns: open an issue on this project's GitHub repository.
